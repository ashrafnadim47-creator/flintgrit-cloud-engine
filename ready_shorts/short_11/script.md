In nineteen ninety five, a Nigerian man sold a completely fake, non-existent airport to an international bank for two hundred forty two million dollars.

Emmanuel Nwude posed as the Governor of the Central Bank of Nigeria and contacted the director of Brazil's Banco Noroeste.

He convinced the bank to invest in a massive international airport in the new capital of Abuja, promising ten million dollars in instant kickbacks.

Over three years, the Brazilian banker wired two hundred forty two million dollars into secret overseas accounts.

The scam only unraveled when a Spanish bank tried to buy Banco Noroeste, and their auditors asked why half of the bank's entire capital was sitting in an airport nobody could find on a map.

It remains the third largest banking fraud in global history.

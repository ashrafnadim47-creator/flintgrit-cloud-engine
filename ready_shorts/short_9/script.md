In nineteen sixty two, three convicts escaped America's most inescapable fortress prison using sharpened spoons.

For six straight months, they patiently scraped away crumbling concrete behind their cell vents.

To fool guards on night patrol, they sculpted fake dummy heads out of soap, toilet paper, and real human hair from the prison barber shop.

After stuffing the fake heads into their beds, they slipped into the utility corridor and climbed to the roof.

On the dark shoreline, they pumped up an inflatable raft secretly stitched from fifty stolen raincoats.

They vanished into the freezing San Francisco bay and were never seen again.

The FBI spent seventeen years hunting them down, but the spoon-wielding trio had officially pulled off the impossible.

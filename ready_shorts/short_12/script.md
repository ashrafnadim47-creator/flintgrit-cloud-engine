In two thousand eleven, a gang of Canadian thieves stole eighteen million dollars worth of pure maple syrup in the stickiest heist in human history.

Deep in rural Quebec, the Global Strategic Maple Syrup Reserve held thousands of white barrels, guarding seventy percent of the planet's entire syrup supply.

Over several months, the crew secretly hauled barrels away by truck, drained the liquid gold into giant plastic vats, refilled the barrels with tap water, and put them back.

The scheme went undetected until an inspector climbed a towering stack of barrels, leaned against one, and almost fell because the barrel was completely empty.

Nearly ten thousand barrels were sucked dry before the police caught the maple syrup mafia.

It remains the most absurd multi-million dollar heist ever recorded.

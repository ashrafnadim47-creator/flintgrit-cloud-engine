In two thousand three, an Italian crew broke into the world's most secure diamond vault using a can of women's hairspray.

Located two floors beneath Antwerp, the vault was guarded by ten layers of high-tech security, including seismic detectors and Doppler radar.

To bypass the infrared heat sensors, the thieves coated the lens with ordinary hairspray, blinding its thermal vision.

They taped magnetic sensors together with aluminum strips so the alarms never triggered when the giant steel door swung open.

Inside, they emptied over one hundred safety deposit boxes, stuffing hundred million dollars of loose diamonds into leather duffel bags.

They walked out into the cold night without firing a single bullet or tripping a single wire.

It remains the largest and cleanest diamond heist in human history.
